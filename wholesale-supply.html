OUR REACH

Daily Supply to Chandrapur City & Beyond

At Suresh Namdeo Chawre (M/S Parag Sureshrao Chaware HUF), we are the backbone of vegetable supply in Chandrapur district. With 25+ years as a licensed Commission Agent (Aadhathiya) at Krushi Utpanna Bazar Samiti, Ram Nagar, Chandrapur, we ensure fresh, quality-graded vegetables reach every corner of the city — from local kirana stores to large hotels and institutional kitchens.

Our daily mandi operations start at dawn — farmers bring their produce, we grade, auction, and dispatch to buyers across Chandrapur, Ballarpur, Bhadravati, Warora, Rajura, Chimur, Mul and surrounding talukas of Vidarbha.

OUR EDGE

What Sets Us Apart

Five pillars that define our wholesale excellence and build lasting buyer trust in Chandrapur

01 — Quality
Rigorous Quality Control

Multi-level grading and quality inspection at every APMC auction — only fresh, Grade-A vegetables dispatched to buyers every day.

02 — Technology
QR Code Traceability

Every batch carries a QR code linking to detailed farm origin, harvest date, farmer name, and pesticide test results — full transparency at a scan.

03 — Pricing
Fair & Transparent Pricing

As licensed APMC Commission Agents (Aadhathiya), we ensure fair mandi-rate auctioning for both farmers and buyers — no hidden charges, no price manipulation.

04 — Freshness
Farm-to-Market Freshness

Vegetables sourced directly from local Chandrapur and Vidarbha farmers, auctioned same-day at APMC, and dispatched immediately — maximum freshness guaranteed.

05 — Reliability
Year-Round Reliable Supply

25+ years of uninterrupted operations through every season — from summer peaks to monsoon challenges. Our buyers count on us every day, 365 days a year.
