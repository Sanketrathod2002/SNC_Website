Our Vision & Mission

Empowering farmers. Serving buyers. Strengthening Chandrapur.

Our Vision 🎯

Revolutionize Agricultural Trade Through Transparency, Trust & Efficiency

We envision a future where farmers receive fair value, buyers receive fresh traceable produce, and Chandrapur’s agricultural community grows stronger. With technology, trust, and 25+ years of experience, we connect Vidarbha farms with nearby markets.

What We Stand For

Our Mission Pillars

Five commitments that guide every action we take.

01 🌾
Mission 01

Empowering Farmers

Providing fair, transparent market pricing and reliable commission agent (Aadhathiya) support, ensuring farmers receive maximum value for their produce at APMC Chandrapur.

02 🌱
Mission 02

Sustainable Agriculture

Promoting Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) and residue-free cultivation to protect farmer livelihoods, consumer health, and the environment for generations to come.

03 📱
Mission 03

QR Code Traceability

Implementing batch-level QR traceability to connect buyers directly with farm origin and handling data — giving every stakeholder complete transparency and confidence.

04 🌍
Mission 04

Vidarbha Distribution

Strengthening daily distribution across Chandrapur city and nearby talukas, including Ballarpur, Bhadravati, Warora, Rajura, and Chimur, with consistent quality and reliable service.

05 ✅
Mission 05

Quality & Buyer Trust

Maintaining rigorous quality control, consistent grading, and long-term wholesale partnerships built on reliability, transparency, and continuous improvement.
